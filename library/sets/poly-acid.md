# Set: poly-acid

Built 2026-11-13 by `tr8s rework` · 16 patterns · about 66 minutes · bank 8, kits 113-128

## Rig

- TR-8S only, no laptop. The set is bank 8: hold PTN SELECT, press pad 8, release, then pads 1-16 are the set.
- Each pattern 8-NN plays its own kit (8-01 → kit 113 … 8-16 → kit 128) and its own tempo. That needs UTILITY → GENERAL: KitSel = PTN and TempoSrc = PTN (setup below).
- A new pattern starts when the playing one returns to its first step: press the next pad during the last bar.
- Variations change by hand: press the next one during the bar before. Hold one and press others to chain them.

## One-time setup on the panel

1. UTILITY → GENERAL: **KitSel = PTN**, **TempoSrc = PTN** (so each pattern brings its kit and BPM). Shuffle = PTN too. Then WRITE → System.
2. **MOTION [ON]** lit: several sources carry recorded knob motion, which is silent with MOTION off.
3. AUTO FILL IN: knob on **16**, leave [ON] off until a pattern says so. FILL IN type: the pattern's own fills.
4. Faders: start everything at about 3/4. Level is the fader's alone; nothing written to the machine sets it.
5. CTRL map (hold CTRL SELECT, press the instrument, choose with VALUE; it is the machine's, so once covers every pattern): **MT and HT = filter cutoff** (the acid voices), **SD and RS = delay send** (throws), **CH and OH = decay or the INST FX**, **CC and RC = reverb send**. Then WRITE → System. [check] the exact parameter names in the panel's list.
6. Know your rescue moves: **MUTE + CLEAR** clears every mute; **SHIFT + TEMPO** moves tempo in 0.1 BPM.

### Moves

- **Main:** AUTO FILL IN on (16 bars). Ride the ACCENT knob up over 16 bars before the break.
- **Break:** the kick is out (or MUTE it). Open the REVERB level, throw the DELAY on the snare (raise DELAY LEVEL for one bar, then back). MASTER FX on with a filter for the last 8 bars: sweep the MASTER FX CTRL knob up into the drop.
- **Drop:** MASTER FX off on the 1, everything unmuted, MANUAL TRIG on the bar before.
- **Peak:** chain two peak variations (hold one, press the other) for 2-bar phrases.
